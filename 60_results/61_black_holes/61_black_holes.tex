\subsection{Black Holes: the Two-Parameter Map}
\label{sec:results-bh}

On the held-out $\beta$ curves, the saved surrogate of the two-parameter map $(\beta, \rh)$ holds the mass $M$ to \resultsBhMassTest{} significant figures at the 95th percentile of the relative error, against \resultsBhMassCeiling{} figures for the data ceiling: it falls \resultsBhMassGap{} figures short.
The dilaton charge $D$ reaches \resultsBhChargeTest{} figures against a ceiling of \resultsBhChargeCeiling{}, \resultsBhChargeGap{} figures short.
Both targets use a \resultsBhMassFamily{} surrogate fitted \resultsBhMassUnit{}.

Figure~\ref{fig:results-bh-errors} shows where the error sits.
It rises toward the existence edge $r_{h,\min}(\beta)$, by more than four orders of magnitude between $\rh \approx 7.5$ and the edge, and it is flat, at the level of the ceiling, for $\rh$ above about $7.5$.
The candidates and their tuning are in the appendix (Sections~\ref{sec:families} and~\ref{sec:precision}).
