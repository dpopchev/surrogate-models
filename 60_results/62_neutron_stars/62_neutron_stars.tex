\subsection{Neutron Stars: the Three-Parameter Map}
\label{sec:results-ns}

On the held-out $(\beta, \lambda)$ curves, the saved surrogate of the three-parameter map $(\beta, \lambda, \rho_c)$ holds the mass $M$ to \resultsNsMassTest{} significant figures at the 95th percentile of the relative error, against \resultsNsMassCeiling{} figures for the data ceiling: it falls \resultsNsMassGap{} figures short.
The dilaton charge $D$ reaches \resultsNsChargeTest{} figures against a ceiling of \resultsNsChargeCeiling{}, \resultsNsChargeGap{} figures short.
The mass uses a \resultsNsMassFamily{} surrogate fitted \resultsNsMassUnit{}, and the charge a \resultsNsChargeFamily{} one fitted \resultsNsChargeUnit{}.

Figure~\ref{fig:results-ns-errors} shows where the error sits.
For $M$ it rises at low central density, by several orders of magnitude toward $\log_{10}\rho_c \approx 14.8$ for the largest $\lambda$, and it stays near the level of the ceiling for $\log_{10}\rho_c$ above about $15.0$.
For $D$ it does not rise along $\rho_c$: each curve keeps its own level, and the spread between curves is wide.
The candidates and their tuning are in the appendix (Sections~\ref{sec:families} and~\ref{sec:precision}).
