\section{Tuning the Survivors and the Numerical Precision They Reach}
\label{sec:precision}
\InputIfFileExists{99_precision_precision_num}{}{}

Each candidate on the short list of Section~\ref{sec:families} is tuned by its own search under a time budget: up to $\knobTuningTrials$ trials of the tree-structured Parzen estimator of Optuna~\citep{Akiba2019_Optuna} over its own hyperparameters, the first ten drawn at random. The interpolant searches its number of neighbours from $\knobSpaceRbfNeighboursLow$ to $\knobSpaceRbfNeighboursHigh$; the nearest-neighbour regressor $k$ from $\knobSpaceKnnKLow$ to $\knobSpaceKnnKHigh$ and its weighting; the Gaussian process its kernel, squared-exponential, Mat\'ern~5/2 or rational quadratic; XGBoost its tree depth, number of trees and learning rate; the residual network, scored at its best point of the earlier search in place of a new one, its width from $\knobSpaceNetWidthLow$ to $\knobSpaceNetWidthHigh$ units, its depth from $\knobSpaceNetDepthLow$ to $\knobSpaceNetDepthHigh$ blocks, its activation, loss and a learning rate from $\knobSpaceNetLrLow$ to $\knobSpaceNetLrHigh$. A curve-wise candidate also searches the knots of its spline, from $\knobSpaceKnotsLow$ to $\knobSpaceKnotsHigh$ against the screen's $\knobFamiliesKnots$, and for the black holes, whose curves hold many more rows, from $\knobSpaceKnotsBhLow$ to $\knobSpaceKnotsBhHigh$. A search stops at its budget of $\knobTuningBudgetMinutes$ minutes, and the pointwise interpolant, which leads a zone but is slow, is scored on $\tunNsMassRbfPointwiseTrials$ fixed neighbour counts in place of a search; \cref{tab:tuning} gives each candidate's verdict beside the trials it finished: searched when its trials ran inside the budget, grid when fixed points were scored in place of a search, cut: too slow when the search ended on the clock. A trial is scored on the frozen folds alone, as the mean of their 95th-percentile figures, so the test curves never steer the search; a fit past the $\knobTuningMinutes$-minute wall of the screen fails its trial. The best trial is refitted through the harness and scored once on the test curves. \Cref{tab:tuning} sets both beside the screen's figures and the data ceiling of each pair.

\InputIfFileExists{99_precision_fig_ranges}{}{}

The $\knobTuningTrials$ trials suffice for every searched family: of the $\tunSearches$ searches, $\tunSearchesNearEarly$ come within $\tunNear$ figures of their best before trial $\tunEarly$.

The tuning moves the curve-wise black-hole candidates most and the others little. On the black holes the curve-wise interpolant and Gaussian process each gain several figures, from $\tunBhMassRbfCurvewiseScreen$ to $\tunBhMassRbfCurvewiseFolds$ on the mass and from $\tunBhChargeRbfCurvewiseScreen$ to $\tunBhChargeRbfCurvewiseFolds$ and $\tunBhChargeGprCurvewiseFolds$ on the charge, and all four settle near the top of the widened knots' range: $\tunBhMassRbfCurvewiseKnots$, $\tunBhMassGprCurvewiseKnots$, $\tunBhChargeRbfCurvewiseKnots$ and $\tunBhChargeGprCurvewiseKnots$ knots against the most allowed, $\knobSpaceKnotsBhHigh$. The pointwise black-hole candidates gain little, the interpolant $\tunBhChargeRbfPointwiseGain$ figures on the charge and XGBoost $\tunBhMassXgboostPointwiseGain$ and $\tunBhChargeXgboostPointwiseGain$, and the pointwise interpolant takes $\tunBhChargeRbfPointwiseNeighbours$ neighbours on the charge against the fewest allowed, $\knobSpaceRbfNeighboursLow$. On the neutron stars the interpolant gains at most a tenth of a figure in either unit; pointwise its best of the fixed points is the most neighbours allowed, $\tunNsMassRbfPointwiseNeighbours$ on the mass and $\tunNsChargeRbfPointwiseNeighbours$ on the charge of $\knobSpaceRbfNeighboursHigh$ (\cref{fig:tuning-ranges}, left and middle). In the curve-wise searches of the interpolant and the Gaussian process the knots carry most of the variance of the trials' figures, an importance of $\tunNsMassRbfCurvewiseImportanceKnots$ to $\tunBhMassRbfCurvewiseImportanceKnots$ in the functional analysis of variance of Optuna, against at most $\tunNsMassRbfCurvewiseImportanceNeighbours$ for the base model's own setting; the curve-wise nearest neighbours owe theirs to $k$ instead, $\tunNsChargeKnnCurvewiseImportanceK$ against $\tunNsChargeKnnCurvewiseImportanceKnots$ for the knots.

The residual network gains most among the neutron-star candidates, from $\tunNsChargeResnetPointwiseScreen$ to $\tunNsChargeResnetPointwiseFolds$ figures on the charge, scored at its recorded best point ($\tunNsChargeResnetPointwiseTrials$ point, verdict $\tunNsChargeResnetPointwiseVerdict$) of $\tunNsChargeResnetPointwiseWidth$ units over $\tunNsChargeResnetPointwiseDepth$ blocks and a learning rate of $\tunNsChargeResnetPointwiseLr$, and on the test curves it keeps $\tunNsChargeResnetPointwiseTest$, level with the curve-wise interpolant's $\tunNsChargeRbfCurvewiseTest$. No fit of it passes the wall in this batch ($\tunNsChargeResnetPointwiseWalls$ walled), where its search of the earlier batch held the tuning for hours.

Section~\ref{sec:results-zones} sets the masses and charges after the tuning against their data ceilings.

A curve-wise search takes at most $\tunNsChargeRbfCurvewiseSearchMinutes$ minutes, every fit on one thread.

This tuning is a first pass: the next one widens the knots above $\knobSpaceKnotsBhHigh$ and the neighbours below $\knobSpaceRbfNeighboursLow$ and above $\knobSpaceRbfNeighboursHigh$, where best trials sit on the edge of their range.

\FloatBarrier

The precision regime runs on the tuned winner of each pair of its own recorded batch, scored on the frozen folds and the test curves with $\precSeeds$ seeds, and an option is kept only when its gain in figures exceeds the spread of the tuned option over folds and seeds. All four winners of that batch are local interpolants, so double precision and the L-BFGS stage, which concern the networks, do not apply to them, and the one option scored is the residual stage, a second interpolant fitted to the residuals of the first. On the neutron stars it gains nothing: the mass stays at $\precNsMassResidualFolds$ figures against $\precNsMassTunedFolds$ tuned, a change of $\precNsMassGain$ within the spread of $\precNsMassSpread$, and the charge at $\precNsChargeResidualFolds$ against $\precNsChargeTunedFolds$, $\precNsChargeGain$ within $\precNsChargeSpread$, so the tuned winner is kept for both. On the black holes it gains $\precBhMassGain$ figures on the mass, from $\precBhMassTunedFolds$ to $\precBhMassResidualFolds$, and $\precBhChargeGain$ on the charge, from $\precBhChargeTunedFolds$ to $\precBhChargeResidualFolds$, each against a spread of at most $\precBhMassSpread$, so the residual stage is kept for both. Against the data ceilings the kept figures leave a gap: $\precNsMassTunedFolds$ against $\nsEdaCeilMFigures$ and $\precNsChargeTunedFolds$ against $\nsEdaCeilDFigures$ on the neutron stars, $\precBhMassResidualFolds$ against $\bhEdaCeilMFigures$ and $\precBhChargeResidualFolds$ against $\bhEdaCeilDFigures$ on the black holes. Hypothesis~$H_2$ is not tested here, since no pair winner is a network.
