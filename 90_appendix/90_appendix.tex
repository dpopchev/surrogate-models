\appendix

This appendix holds the data science behind the results of the paper, in the order in which the work was done. It begins with the data: the exploratory analysis of the neutron-star and black-hole maps, the accuracy any surrogate can reach on each, the preprocessing decisions and the hypotheses the search tests. It then gives the methodology: the baseline network and the pipeline every model runs through, and the harness that scores them. It ends with the results of the search: the representation of inputs and targets, the model families screened and how they were eliminated, and the tuning and numerical precision of the survivors. Each section says in its title what to look up there.
