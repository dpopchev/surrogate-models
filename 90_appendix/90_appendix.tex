\appendix

This appendix holds the data science behind the results of the paper, in the order in which the work was done. It begins with the data: the exploratory analysis of the neutron-star and black-hole maps, the accuracy any surrogate can reach on each, and the preprocessing decisions. It then gives the methodology: the harness that scores the models. It ends with the results of the search: the representation of inputs and targets, the model families screened and how they were eliminated, and the tuning and numerical precision of the survivors. Each section says in its title what to look up there.
