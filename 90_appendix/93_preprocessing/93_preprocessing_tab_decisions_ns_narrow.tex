\textbf{Main input} &
$\log_{10}\rhoc$, standardized. Positive and multiplicative; $M$ follows it as one smooth curve per $(\beta, \lambda)$ pair; skew $\nsEdaRhocRawSkew \to \nsEdaRhocLogSkew$, supporting only. &
$\rh$, linear, standardized. Uniform grid of step $\bhEdaRhStep$ over $\bhEdaRhOrders$ orders of magnitude, skew $\bhEdaRhRawSkew$; a logarithm would only add skew. \\
\textbf{Coupling inputs} &
$\beta, \lambda$ linear, standardized, \emph{both retained}. A straight line in $\lambda$ explains $\nsEdaLambdaLinearPercentLogDM\%$ of the charge target's variance; at fixed $\lambda$, $\beta$ explains $\nsEdaBetaSharePercent\%$: they interact. &
$\beta$ linear, standardized, retained. At fixed $\rh$ it moves $M$ by $\bhEdaBetaEffectPercentM\%$ and $\log_{10}\Dch$ by $\bhEdaBetaEffectPercentLD\%$ of their spread, but it sets the existence edge, $\rh^{\min}$ from $\bhEdaEdgeRhMinLow$ to $\bhEdaEdgeRhMinHigh$. \\
\textbf{Charge target} &
$\log_{10}(\Dch/M)$. $\Dch$ spans $\nsEdaDOrders$ orders of magnitude, every value positive; dividing by $M$ lowers the Spearman correlation with $M$ from $\nsEdaCorrMLogD$ to $\nsEdaCorrMLogDM$ and raises the Pearson correlation with $\lambda$ from $\nsEdaCorrLambdaLogD$ to $\nsEdaCorrLambdaLogDM$. &
$\log_{10}(\Dch/M)$, as for neutron stars. Dividing by $M$ brings no gain here (Spearman with $M$: $\bhEdaSpearmanMLogD$ against $\bhEdaSpearmanMLogDM$); kept for one definition across both datasets; smallest $\Dch$ $\bhEdaDMin$. \\
\textbf{Mass target} &
$M$, in $\Msun$. Bounded and of order one (up to $\nsEdaMassMax\,\Msun$); $\nsEdaPileUpPercent\%$ of rows lie within $\nsEdaPileUpWindow\,\Msun$ of their curve's maximum, a sampling artefact, disclosed. &
$M$, in $\Msun$. Bounded and of order one ($\bhEdaMMin$ to $\bhEdaMMax\,\Msun$); follows $\rh$ with Pearson $r = \bhEdaPearsonMRh$; $\beta$ moves it by $\bhEdaBetaEffectPercentM\%$ at fixed $\rh$. \\
\textbf{Input scaling} &
Standardization fitted on the training folds only. The inputs differ in scale by orders of magnitude; fitting on training folds only keeps held-out curves out of the model. &
Standardization fitted on the training folds only, as for neutron stars. \\
\textbf{Split} &
$\prepNsTestPercent\%$ of curves frozen as test; GroupKFold ($\prepNsFolds$ folds) over $(\beta, \lambda)$ curves. $\nsEdaSameCurvePercent\%$ of rows have their nearest neighbour on their own curve; a row-random split scores $\nsEdaSplitRandomRowsDM$ against $\nsEdaSplitCurvesDM$ for whole curves. &
$\prepBhTestCurves$ of $\prepBhCurves$ curves frozen as test; GroupKFold ($\prepBhFolds$ folds) over $\beta$ curves. $\bhEdaSameCurvePercent\%$ of rows have their nearest neighbour on their own curve; for $\log_{10}\Dch$ a row-random split scores $\bhEdaSplitRandomRowsLD$ against $\bhEdaSplitCurvesLD$ for whole curves. \\
\textbf{Domain of validity} &
The filled $(\beta, \lambda)$ triangle and the sampled $\rhoc$ range; no extrapolation claimed. $\nsEdaGridFillPercent\%$ grid fill; predicting the rim from the interior scores $\nsEdaSplitRimDM$ (ablation). &
$\beta$ from $\bhEdaEdgeBetaLow$ to $\bhEdaEdgeBetaHigh$, $\rh$ from the existence edge to $\bhEdaRhMax$. The outermost curves score as any other ($\bhEdaSplitOuterCurvesLD$, ablation). \\
