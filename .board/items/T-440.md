A new pure module on numpy, paperlib/ceilings.py and paperlib/scorecard.py only. curve_position(groups, along): per row its rank along its own curve divided by the row count of the curve less one, 0 at the smallest value of along and 1 at the largest; a one-row curve gives 0. physical_edge(position, side, fraction): the boolean rows within fraction of the start of their curve (side start: the black-hole existence edge) or of its end (side end: the neutron-star stable edge). zone_masks(ablation, position, side, fraction): a mapping of three boolean masks, interior (ablation False), edge (ablation True) and physical (physical_edge). zone_figures(errors, masks): per zone the significant figures of the p95 and of the worst row (paperlib/ceilings.py spread, paperlib/scorecard.py significant_figures), None for an empty zone. RED first: test_zone_figures_report_p95_and_worst_row_per_zone on a tiny synthetic array of two curves of five rows asserts the three masks, both figures of each zone and None for an empty zone.
