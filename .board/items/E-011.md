## Hypothesis
Given 1 to 3 variants of every figure and table rendered side by side under the shared style, does the developer pick one for each, recorded in paper.toml, so that make compile builds the paper with the picks and every number Sections 4.1 to 5.3 and the appendix cite comes from a generated macro? Refuted if a variant cannot be expressed as a typed choice in paper.toml, or a pick changes a figure the text does not place.

## Outcome
make variants writes every variant of every figure and table under build/variants/<section>/ with one contact sheet per asset, paper.toml records the pick per figure and table, make compile renders the picks, and Sections 4.1 to 5.3 and the appendix cite their numbers through macros.

## Acceptance criteria
- AC1 Given a [results.families] figures list in paper.toml, When make assets runs, Then only the listed 5.2 figures and their wrappers are written and the paper compiles without the others -> W-102
- AC2 Given paper.toml naming a variant of the black-hole mass_radius figure, When make variants runs, Then build/variants/42_black_holes/ holds every variant of it with one contact sheet and make compile places the picked one -> W-111
- AC3 Given the Section 3.1 and 3.2 figures, When make variants runs, Then each is rendered in its 2 to 3 variants with a contact sheet -> W-112
- AC4 Given the Section 4.1, 5.2 and 5.3 figures, When make variants runs, Then each is rendered in its 2 to 3 variants with a contact sheet -> W-113
- AC5 Given the tables of Sections 3.1 to 5.3, When make variants runs, Then each is rendered in its 1 to 3 layouts to a page image under build/variants/tables/ -> W-114
- AC6 Given 2 to 3 PlotStyle presets in paper.toml, When make variants runs, Then three representative figures are rendered under each preset on one contact sheet -> W-115
- AC7 Given the result numbers and knobs Sections 4.1 to 5.3 and the appendix cite, When make compile runs, Then every one of them comes from a generated macro and no literal result remains in the text -> W-116
