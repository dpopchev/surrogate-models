## Action
Skill build-with-tdd. Replaces the other half of the pruned T-132, as a harness fitter rather than a design, so the harness, scorecard and ledger stay unchanged. curvewise_fitter(base, keys, along, knots) -> Fitter: from the fit rows, group by the curve-key columns, map each curve's along column to s in [0, 1] (NS: log10 rho_c over the curve; BH: r_h over its range), fit a cubic spline of the target on fixed knots in s, and fit the base estimator from the keys (plus the curve's along range) to the spline coefficients; predicting a row rebuilds its curve from the predicted coefficients and evaluates it at the row's s. Tests on synthetic curves that are cubic in s: with a base that is exact on the keys, the fitter reproduces the rows to near machine precision; a curve unseen in the fit rows is predicted from its keys.
