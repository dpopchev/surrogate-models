The developer asked for the expected uncertainty they mean on the predicted values -- sigma_M = epsilon M, sigma_D = epsilon D, and epsilon / ln 10 in dex for the charge -- at typical values (the median mass and M_max; the median charge), so they can be set beside observational uncertainties and the MCMC error budget (E-008). ## Hypothesis
Can the data ceilings be stated as absolute uncertainties of M and D at typical values, from generated numbers alone?

## Outcome
Given the data ceilings, When make builds the EDA assets, Then Sections 3.1 and 3.2 state them as absolute uncertainties of M and D at typical values.

## Findings
- 2026-10-07 fact: the NS median mass is 1.75 M_sun, not 1.4: the p95 ceiling means 1.36e-4 M_sun there, 1.70e-4 at M_max 2.19, the charge 1.56e-3 dex; BH 3.05e-7 M_sun at M 3.58, 4.40e-7 dex -> Sections 3.1, 3.2 (T-161)
