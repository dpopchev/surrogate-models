Add the pure slope_violations(y_true, y_pred, groups, along, masks): per zone of masks the pair (violations, steps). The rows of a curve (equal groups) are ordered by along; a step is two rows of one curve next to each other in that order. A step whose true difference is zero is not counted. A step belongs to a zone when both of its rows are in the mask of the zone. A violation is a counted step whose predicted difference has another sign than its true difference, a predicted difference of zero included. A zone with no step gives (0, 0). The values are compared in the form the target took (the y of the design), since log10 keeps the sign of a slope.
