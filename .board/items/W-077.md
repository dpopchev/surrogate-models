## Hypothesis
Can every harness run leave a record from which the search's best-so-far accuracy against fits and against compute can be rebuilt without refitting?

## Outcome
Given a screen run through the harness, When the developer runs make ledger, Then every fit of it appears as one row with its candidate, settings, seed, fold and test significant figures and fit seconds.
