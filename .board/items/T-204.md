## Action
Skill build-with-tdd. The tuning (T-205) builds the same candidates as the family screen, but a runnable module never imports another's code, so the screen's candidate vocabulary and fitters move from 60_results/62_families/screen_families.py to shared/candidates.py: Family, FAMILIES, Unit, UNITS, Candidate, Knobs, fitter_of, _factory, _with_skips, curve_columns and CurveNet. screen_families.py imports them from there and keeps its behavior; the fitter_of tests in 60_results/62_families/test_screen_families.py move to shared/test_candidates.py. Unit is the one in shared/search.py (T-136), imported, not defined twice. New behavior, one RED test each: tuned_fitter(candidate, space, knobs, training, params) -> Fitter lays one trial's params (the names of shared/search.py SPACES) over the knobs and the training -- k, neighbours and knots onto Knobs; width, depth, activation, loss and lr onto Training; the k-NN's weights, the GPR's kernel (rbf, matern52 as Matern nu 2.5, rational_quadratic) and XGBoost's max_depth, n_estimators and learning_rate onto the built estimator by set_params. Tests on tiny synthetic params only, e.g. a k-NN trial with weights uniform builds a uniform k-NN, a curve-wise trial's knots set its spline's knots.
