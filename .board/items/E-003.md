## Hypothesis
Can one top-level paper.toml, read by pydantic-settings into typed section models in shared/, choose which exploratory figures each Section 4 subsection renders for the NS and the BH dataset, in one shared style whose colors tell an NS figure from a BH one, so make regenerates the paper with the chosen figures for review? Refuted if a figure variant or style value cannot be expressed as a typed field, an invalid value loads without error, or an NS figure cannot be told from a BH one by its colors.

## Outcome
shared/plots.py and shared/config.py give a typed PaperConfig and one dataset-aware plot style; Section 4 holds an NS EDA, a BH EDA, a preprocessing-decisions and a hypotheses subsection; the NS and BH EDA scripts beside their sections write the figures paper.toml selects into build/assets, and make compile includes them.

## Acceptance criteria
- AC1 Given a paper.toml, When shared.config.load_config() reads it, Then a typed PaperConfig is returned only when every key names an existing section folder and every value is an allowed choice -> W-013
- AC2 Given a PlotStyle, When shared.plots.apply_style(style) runs, Then the rcParams and the per-dataset colormaps in effect are the ones the PlotStyle configures -> W-014
- AC3 Given the restructured 40_data_analysis tree, When make compile runs, Then Section 4 holds the NS EDA, BH EDA, preprocessing decisions and hypotheses subsections in that order and the PDF builds -> W-016
- AC4 Given local/state/neutron_stars.parquet and paper.toml, When make compile runs, Then Section 4.1 shows every NS EDA figure paper.toml selects, generated by 40_data_analysis/41_neutron_stars/eda_neutron_stars.py into build/assets -> W-017
- AC5 Given local/state/black_holes.parquet and paper.toml, When make compile runs, Then Section 4.2 shows every BH EDA figure paper.toml selects, generated by 40_data_analysis/42_black_holes/eda_black_holes.py into build/assets -> W-018

## Decisions
- 2026-10-05: paper.toml is the entry point that chooses which figures and computations are produced; each choice is a Literal scoped to what its module can draw or compute, so a new variant is added to the module and its Literal together
- 2026-10-05: values live in a top-level paper.toml, the schema in shared/config.py (pydantic-settings TOML source, env overrides PAPER__<SECTION>__<SUBSECTION>__<FIELD>); section keys are the folder names without their number prefix
- 2026-10-05: one shared matplotlib and seaborn style for every figure; text rendered with usetex (pdflatex, Latin Modern) to match the 11pt article; default palette colorblind
- 2026-10-05: the first slice is the config skeleton plus one real choice; further choices land with the figure or table that consumes them
- 2026-10-05: every figure and table series is one curve of fixed free parameters cut at its M_max (W-008 rule); figures and tables show the variation around M_max
- 2026-10-05: EDA per dataset, to organize the split from the data; scripts beside their section, figures built into build/assets and chosen in paper.toml, never in local/scratch
- 2026-10-05: NS and BH use distinct color families, so a beta colorbar tells the dataset; BH and NS may not share a theory
- 2026-10-05: colors: NS cool (lambda crest, beta mako, anchor blue), BH warm (beta flare, anchor orange)
- 2026-10-05: paper says orders of magnitude, never decades
