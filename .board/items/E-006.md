## Hypothesis
Within one search space -- representation, model family, hyperparameters, training regime, data budget -- does a surrogate come within one significant figure of the data's interpolation ceiling (p95 relative error on held-out interior curves) for each dataset x target pair, and which candidate is the cheapest to fit and to call? Refuted for a pair when no candidate comes within one figure of its ceiling; a refuted pair is reported, never hidden.

## Outcome
For the two maps the paper learns -- black holes (beta, r_h) -> (M, D) and neutron stars (rho_c, beta, lambda) -> (M, D), one model per target, D rebuilt as M 10^Y -- make targets write the reachable relative error per dataset into Sections 3.1 and 3.2, score every candidate on one scorecard (significant figures at median, p95 and max per zone and per decade of D, ripple, fit and inference seconds at one thread), and end in Section 5's Pareto front naming the best approach per pair, with its extrapolation error against the distance from the training hull.
