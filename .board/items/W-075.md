## Hypothesis
Can M_max be predicted for given (beta, lambda) to within its own data ceiling, and is a model of its own better than the maximum of the mass surrogate?

## Outcome
Given the per-curve M_max ceiling and the derived and the direct M_max predictions on the held-out curves, When the developer weighs them, Then whether and how the surrogate predicts M_max is decided on this spike.
