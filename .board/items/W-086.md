## Hypothesis
Does make_rbf predict every query when its neighbourhood alone would leave the thin-plate's polynomial part rank-deficient?

## Outcome
Given training rows on two parallel lines in three inputs, When make_rbf(n) with n no more than those rows predicts a point between the lines, Then it returns a finite value instead of raising LinAlgError.

## Decisions
- 2026-10-08: a planar neighbourhood falls back per query to scipy's linear kernel at degree 0 (always full rank); cubic needs degree 1 in scipy 1.18 (developer: per-query fallback)

## Findings
- 2026-10-08 T-194: fact: rbf(50) was singular on 5/10 NS folds; with the fallback rbf(100) runs on all 18 harness fits, 10-74 s each -> the screen runs at 100 supersedes: W-066#1
