  steps: run the representation screen on the prepared NS table with --knots 8, or fit curvewise_fitter(..., knots=8) on rows holding a curve of fewer than 10 rows
  technical: make_lsq_spline needs at least as many rows as coefficients (Schoenberg-Whitney); the base regressor needs one output length per curve, so a short curve cannot simply take fewer knots. A minimum-norm least-squares fit of the B-spline design matrix (BSpline.design_matrix, numpy lstsq) gives the full coefficient vector for an underdetermined curve and the same answer as make_lsq_spline for a determined one.

## Hypothesis
Does a minimum-norm spline fit let every curve, however short, give the base its full coefficient vector?

## Outcome
Given fit rows holding a curve with fewer rows than the spline's coefficients, When the curve-wise fitter fits them, Then it returns a predictor and the rows of the longer curves are reproduced as before.

## Decisions
- 2026-10-08: a curve with fewer rows than coefficients gets the minimum-norm least-squares spline; knots stay 8 (developer)
