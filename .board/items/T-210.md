## Action
Skill build-with-tdd. Three options, each a function returning a new Fitter or estimator, the data untouched: (1) float64 for the network -- make_estimator(training, ..., dtype=torch.float64) casts the module and the arrays end to end, network_fitter passes it through; (2) an L-BFGS stage -- after the AdamW fit stops, a second skorch fit of the same module with torch.optim.LBFGS (closure through skorch's train_step) for a bounded number of steps, kept only when the validation loss falls; (3) a residual stage for any family -- residual_fitter(first: Fitter, second: Fitter) -> Fitter in shared/candidates.py fits second on first's residuals of the fit rows and sums the predictions. Tests on the toy smooth function of test_surrogate.py with toy epochs: float64 keeps the dtype of the parameters and the predictions; the L-BFGS stage does not raise the validation loss; the residual stage does not raise the training error of a toy k-NN. Context: W-068's open Finding (which precision fits RBF, GPR, XGBoost): float64 and the residual stage apply to every family, L-BFGS to the networks only.

## Output
The three precision options in shared/surrogate.py and shared/candidates.py with their tests.
