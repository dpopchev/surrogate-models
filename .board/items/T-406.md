## Action
Files: paperlib/domain.py (new), paperlib/test_domain.py (new). Skills model-domain and build-with-tdd. Pure, no I/O. BlackHoleDomain: per sampled beta the smallest and the largest r_h of its curve (the smallest is the existence edge r_h,min), built by make_black_hole_domain(table) from the columns beta and r_h. NeutronStarDomain: per sampled (beta, lambda) the smallest rho_c and the rho_c of the last row of its curve (the prepared table is cut at the maximum mass, so that row is the stable edge), built by make_neutron_star_domain(table) from the columns beta, lambda and rho_c. Between sampled curves an edge is interpolated linearly in the curve keys (beta; (beta, lambda)); outside the sampled keys nothing is interpolated. Verdict is a sum type: Inside, or Outside(reason, nearest) where reason names the edge crossed (coupling range, existence edge, stable branch, sampled range) and nearest is the closest valid input: the couplings clipped into their sampled range, then the curve coordinate clipped into the range of that curve. check(domain, x) returns the Verdict of one input row; matched exhaustively with assert_never. RED first: test_query_below_the_existence_edge_is_outside builds a toy black-hole table of two beta curves and asserts that a query with r_h below the edge of its beta returns Outside with the reason existence edge. Further single-assert tests record: a query on a sampled row is Inside; the nearest of a refused query is Inside; a neutron-star query above the rho_c of the stable edge is Outside.

## Output
paperlib/domain.py with paperlib/test_domain.py: BlackHoleDomain and NeutronStarDomain built from a prepared table, and check(domain, x) returning Inside or Outside(reason, nearest).
