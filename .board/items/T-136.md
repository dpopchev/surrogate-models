## Action
Skill build-with-tdd. search(family, space, budget) runs Optuna (uv add optuna) with the same number of trials per family, each trial scored by the harness on an inner GroupKFold of the training curves (never the test curves), the objective the p95 significant figures. Per-family spaces: networks width, depth, activation (ReLU, GELU, SiLU, tanh), loss (MSE, Huber), lr and schedule; GPR kernel; spline order; k-NN k and weights; XGBoost depth and rounds. Tests with a toy factory and a 3-trial budget: trials never see a test curve, the budget is honoured.
