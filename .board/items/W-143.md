## Hypothesis
Can paperlib/tables.py refuse, at construction, a Table whose header or body row spans more or fewer columns than it has, and a Span narrower than one column, instead of failing later in render or rendering a short row silently?

## Outcome
Given a Table built with a body row one cell short of its columns, When make_table is called with it, Then it raises ValueError naming the row and the two widths
