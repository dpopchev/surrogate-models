## Hypothesis
Does refined training of the architecture E-006 chose (AdamW with a cosine schedule, L-BFGS only if it pays) plus an ensemble over curve-bootstrap resamples give a final surrogate per dataset whose ensemble spread tracks its actual error? Refuted if the ensemble sigma does not rank the test errors.

## Outcome
make surrogate writes the final ensemble per dataset and target under local/state/models/ (interim, rebuilt from the data or provided) and a loader returns (M, D, sigma) for new inputs; Section 4.2 states the training recipe and Section 4.3 shows sigma against the actual error.

- AC2 Given an ensemble sized from the measured fit time, When make runs the ensemble, Then Section 5.3 shows the ensemble sigma against the actual test error -> W-045
