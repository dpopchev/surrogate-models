## Hypothesis
Can one scikit-learn pipeline around a skorch MLP, trained on the frozen curve-grouped split, score each of the four dataset x target pairs beside the mean and nearest-curve reference predictors on the same held-out curves, while it reports its progress live? Refuted if a pair cannot be scored on the same curves as its references, or a validation curve also appears in training.

## Outcome
make baseline fits the four dataset x target pairs with live per-epoch progress and writes the Section 5.1 baseline table (test and fold MARE beside the mean and nearest-curve references), the NS mass parity figure, the charge MARE rebuilt with the true and the predicted mass, and one diagnostics folder per run under local/state/51_algorithms/.
