\subsection{The Relative Error and the Data Ceilings}
\label{sec:error-metric}

Every candidate is scored by one number: the significant figures it keeps at the 95th percentile of the relative error on the interior test curves, set against the data ceiling. Two figures are reported beside the score and do not enter it: those on the edge curves of the coupling grid, held out of a refit, which measure how far the candidate extrapolates, and those on the rows at the physical edge, the existence edge of black holes and the stability edge of neutron stars (\cref{tab:zones}). The data ceiling is the error that the neighbouring curves already allow: a smooth interpolation between them reproduces a held-out curve to that error and no better (Section~\ref{sec:hypotheses}). It is the input that fixes the target of the pipeline, a surrogate within one significant figure of it.

\cref{fig:ns-ceilings,fig:bh-ceilings} show the ceiling of each dataset along its own curves: the error of the interpolation between neighbouring curves, the scale against which every result of Section~\ref{sec:results} is read.
